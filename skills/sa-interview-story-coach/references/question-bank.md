# Interview Question Bank

Use one question at a time in Mock Interview Mode.

## Sales / GTM

1. Tell me about a time you partnered closely with an Account Executive and your technical contribution moved an opportunity forward.
2. Walk me through how you conduct discovery with a new customer.
3. Tell me about a time discovery changed the solution you recommended.
4. Tell me about a technical blocker that threatened an opportunity. What did you do?
5. Tell me about a time you and an AE disagreed.
6. Tell me about a time you had to push back on Sales because the proposed solution created too much technical or customer risk.
7. Tell me about a POC or demo that gave a customer enough confidence to make a decision.
8. How do you know when you've done enough technical validation for a customer to move forward?
9. Tell me about a time you simplified a complex technical solution for an executive stakeholder.
10. Tell me about a time you balanced what the customer wanted with what was best for the business.
11. Tell me about a time you uncovered an expansion or cross-sell opportunity through discovery.
12. Tell me about a time you turned a one-off customer solution into a repeatable sales playbook.
13. Tell me about a deal you lost or failed to advance despite good technical work. What did you learn?

## Product / EPD

1. Tell me about a time you had to bring a messy set of customer signals to Product. How did you determine the real problem?
2. Tell me about a time customer feedback changed your recommendation.
3. Tell me about a time a customer requested a feature but you concluded the underlying need was different.
4. How do you determine whether a customer problem is isolated or systemic?
5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with Product.
6. Tell me about a time Product would not build something an important customer wanted.
7. Tell me about a time you had to prioritize several customer problems under a deadline.
8. Tell me about a time you operated with high agency because ownership was unclear.
9. Tell me about a product or DX intervention you made. How did you know it worked?
10. Tell me about a time you had to balance competing stakeholder priorities. Focus on the product trade-off.
11. Tell me about a time a prototype or direct customer observation exposed a product gap.
12. Tell me about a time you advocated hard for a customer and then decided to stop pushing.
13. Tell me about a time you had incomplete evidence but still had to make a recommendation.

## Technical / Architecture

1. Tell me about an architecture decision where there was no obviously correct answer.
2. Describe a system you designed for scale. What became the bottleneck first?
3. Tell me about a failure mode you discovered before production.
4. How do you design graceful degradation around an unreliable dependency?
5. Describe a migration where you deliberately avoided a big-bang rewrite.
6. Tell me about a time data locality or latency changed your architecture.
7. Tell me about a time security or compliance constraints changed your design.
8. Tell me about a system where observability materially changed how you operated it.
