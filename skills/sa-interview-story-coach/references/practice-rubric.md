# Practice Review Rubric

## Summary table

Start a multi-question review with:

| Interview | Question / competency tested | Story used | Story fit | Answer quality | Better story available? | Biggest improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Per-question review

### Question

**What the interviewer is testing:**
Explain the competency and likely functional lens.

**Assessment of the answer:**
Give a concise overall assessment. Optionally score 1-10.

### 🟢 What worked
- Relevance
- Clear ownership
- Evidence
- Judgment
- Technical / commercial / product signal

### 🟡 What weakened the answer
- Excess context
- Missing decision
- Missing trade-off
- Weak ownership
- Unsupported outcome
- Wrong story
- No reflection

### Story selection
- Original story:
- Fit: Best story / Good story, weak framing / Usable backup / Wrong story
- Better story, if any:
- Why:

### Better retrieval approach
Explain the cue that should fire in real time.

### Improved answer skeleton
- S:
- T:
- A1:
- A2:
- A3:
- Trade-off:
- R:
- Lesson:

### Likely follow-up
One strong pressure-test question.

## Suggested scoring dimensions

Assess separately:

1. Relevance to exact question
2. Situation/context clarity
3. Individual ownership
4. Customer context
5. Business context
6. Technical depth
7. Discovery
8. Judgment
9. Trade-offs
10. Stakeholder management
11. Product thinking
12. Sales/GTM thinking
13. Evidence
14. Result
15. Reflection
16. Target-role alignment
