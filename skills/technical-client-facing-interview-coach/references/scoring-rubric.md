# Scoring Rubric

Score each answer in three dimensions.

## Architecture / Reasoning — 10 points

**9–10:** Clear assumptions, correct request/data flow, meaningful tradeoffs, failure
handling, and validation. Anticipates second-order effects.

**7–8:** Fundamentally sound, misses one important operational or tradeoff detail.

**5–6:** Correct direction but vague, product-led rather than requirement-led, or
missing data flow/failure handling.

**3–4:** Several conceptual errors or unclear architecture.

**1–2:** Mostly guessing or cannot establish a workable model.

## Product / Company Accuracy — 10 points

**9–10:** Maps requirements to verified capabilities precisely and knows key limits.

**7–8:** Correct product mapping with minor gaps.

**5–6:** Knows the broad product family but mixes terminology or overclaims.

**3–4:** Product names are guessed or capabilities are conflated.

**1–2:** Major inaccuracies.

If vendor facts cannot be verified, do not fabricate a score of certainty. Mark the
specific claim "needs verification."

## Communication — 10 points

**9–10:** Starts with a crisp thesis, asks focused discovery questions, uses clear
structure, and ties back to customer/business impact.

**7–8:** Understandable and consultative, with some extra wording.

**5–6:** Meandering, hedged, or missing a top-line recommendation.

**3–4:** Difficult to follow.

**1–2:** No clear recommendation.

## Diagnostic labels

Attach one or more labels:

- CONCEPT_GAP
- VOCABULARY_GAP
- RECALL_GAP
- COMMUNICATION_GAP
- PRODUCT_GAP
- TRADEOFF_GAP
- FAILURE_MODE_GAP
- VALIDATION_GAP

Use labels to decide what to practice next.
